# DataConsentGuard Example Consent Terms

Project: Support agent data room

The data owner authorizes the registered AI support agent to inspect consented
helpdesk tickets only for the purpose of answering customer support questions.

Allowed use:

- Summarize or retrieve facts needed to answer a support request.
- Use no more than 500 ticket records under this policy.
- Use only records listed in the data manifest.

Not allowed:

- Training a general model.
- Marketing, sales profiling, or unrelated analytics.
- Reusing the data after an access receipt is blocked or exhausted.
