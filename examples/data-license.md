# DataConsentGuard Example Data License

License scope: limited operational support use.

The dataset may be accessed by the registered AI agent when all conditions hold:

- The request purpose matches the policy purpose.
- The requester presents a current consent proof.
- The requested record count is within the remaining policy boundary.
- The output is limited to support answers and audit receipts.

The license does not allow unrestricted redistribution, model training, or
queries outside the policy scope.
